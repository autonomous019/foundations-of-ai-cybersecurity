# Table of Contents

## Chapter 1 — Foundations of AI Risk & Security .......................... *Ipso Lorem pp. 1–22*
Introduces the fundamental principles of AI risk, including emergent behaviors, model unpredictability, data-driven vulnerabilities, and why traditional cybersecurity approaches fail against LLMs. Establishes the conceptual ground for the technical chapters that follow.

## Chapter 2 — The AI Threat Landscape ...................................... *Ipso Lorem pp. 23–44*
Maps the modern threat environment: criminals, nation-states, insiders, autonomous agents, and systemic risks created by connected AI systems. Reviews real-world AI misuse incidents and evolving attack patterns.

## Chapter 3 — How Modern AI Systems Work ................................ *Ipso Lorem pp. 45–70*
Explains transformer architecture, embeddings, attention mechanisms, multimodal processing, and agent tool-use. Provides readers with the technical literacy needed to understand later security and alignment risks.

## Chapter 4 — AI Attack Surfaces & Exploitation ........................... *Ipso Lorem pp. 71–106*
Documents every major attack surface: prompt injection, jailbreaks, multimodal adversarial inputs, memory poisoning, model extraction, and supply-chain compromise. Highlights the growing sophistication of adversarial research.

## Chapter 5 — AI Supply Chain Security ..................................... *Ipso Lorem pp. 107–132*
Analyzes vulnerabilities introduced across the entire AI lifecycle: data collection, annotation, training, model distribution, dependency chains, GPU clusters, and cloud infrastructure. Emphasizes dataset poisoning and model-weight tampering risks.

## Chapter 6 — Model Behavior, Drift & Belief Shifts .................... *Ipso Lorem pp. 133–158*
Draws from research to show how LLM internal beliefs shift under context accumulation. Highlights dangers of belief drift, persuasion susceptibility, and hidden changes in latent reasoning.

## Chapter 7 — Human Misuse, Overtrust & Behavioral Risks ........ *Ipso Lorem pp. 159–188*
Covers the human layer of AI incidents, including employee data leaks, shadow AI, over-reliance, misinterpretation, and blind trust.

## Chapter 8 — AI Profit-Maximization Risks .............................. *Ipso Lorem pp. 189–214*
Explores how AI systems optimized for profit, engagement, or competitive advantage can behave dangerously—even without malicious intent.

## Chapter 9 — Multimodal & Sensor-Based Risks ....................... *Ipso Lorem pp. 215–238*
Discusses vulnerabilities in image, audio, video, and sensor-integrated systems, including multimodal adversarial examples and environmental misinterpretation.

## Chapter 10 — Agentic AI & Tool-Enabled Autonomy ................ *Ipso Lorem pp. 239–266*
Explains how autonomous agents plan, execute multi-step tasks, spawn sub-agents, and manipulate tools. Shows why tool access is the real locus of harm.

## Chapter 11 — Memory, Context & RAG Vulnerabilities .......... *Ipso Lorem pp. 267–294*
Focuses on long-context models, RAG pipelines, memory poisoning, retrieval attacks, and instruction contamination across contextual history.

## Chapter 12 — Adversarial Stress Testing & Red Teaming ........ *Ipso Lorem pp. 295–322*
Outlines structured adversarial testing frameworks: automated jailbreaks, multimodal stress testing, behavioral load testing, AI-vs-AI adversarial evaluation, and MITRE ATLAS.

## Chapter 13 — AI Governance, Safety & Organizational Controls ... *Ipso Lorem pp. 323–364*
Defines governance structures and risk controls needed for safe deployment, including NIST RMF, ISO 42001, oversight boards, access control, auditability, and incident response.

## Chapter 14 — Alignment Strategies & Value-Safe AI .............. *Ipso Lorem pp. 365–408*
Maps the core alignment problems (misgeneralization, reward hacking, deceptive alignment, value conflicts) and surveys training-time, post-training, architectural, and governance-level alignment strategies.

## Chapter 15 — Emergent Capabilities & Scaling Risks ............ *Ipso Lorem pp. 409–442*
Explores unanticipated capabilities that emerge as models scale—long-horizon planning, situational awareness, self-correction loops, and recursive reasoning.

## Chapter 16 — Multi-Agent Systems & Collective Behavior ...... *Ipso Lorem pp. 443–474*
Discusses agent swarms, emergent coordination, unintended interactions, and failure cascades in environments with multiple AI agents.

## Chapter 17 — AI Autonomy & Control ..................................... *Ipso Lorem pp. 475–518*
Explores requirements for interruptibility, constraint architectures, tool-scope limits, sandboxing, safety wrappers, and preventing runaway behavior.

## Chapter 18 — Frontier AI, AGI & Long-Horizon Safety ............ *Ipso Lorem pp. 519–552*
Examines risks associated with frontier-scale models—recursive self-improvement, rapid capability jumps, strategic behavior—and frameworks for AGI oversight.

## Chapter 19 — AI & Societal Transformation ............................. *Ipso Lorem pp. 553–586*
Analyzes AI’s impact on culture, cognition, labor markets, geopolitical dynamics, and information ecosystems.

## Chapter 20 — Future Directions & Research Roadmap .......... *Ipso Lorem pp. 587–620*
Concludes with a blueprint for future research in safety, governance, alignment, agent security, and robust AI development.
